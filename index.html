3. Golden Rules Against Cyber Fraud in India

🚫 No "Digital Arrest" in Law

Under Indian law, no police agency, CBI, or ED conducts interrogations or arrests via video call. Hang up immediately.

⏱️ The Golden Hour (1930)

If money is debited, reporting within 1 hour on 1930 allows I4C to freeze the scammer's bank account before withdrawal.

⚠️ Never Install .APK Files

Scammers send fake "RTO Challan" or "Bank KYC" Android apps (.apk) on WhatsApp to read your OTPs remotely.
